About Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi

Discover the profound life and global spiritual legacy of the founder of Sahaj Yoga.

The Early Years & Freedom Struggle

Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi was born on March 21, 1923, in Chhindwara, Madhya Pradesh, India, into a Christian family of noble descent. Her parents played a key role in India's liberation movement.

As a child, she spent time with Mahatma Gandhi at his ashram. Gandhi recognized her extraordinary spiritual wisdom even at a young age.

The Discovery of Sahaj Yoga (1970)

On May 5, 1970, while meditating on a beach in Nargol, Gujarat, Shri Mataji experienced the opening of the Sahasrara (the seventh chakra). This monumental event made it possible for humanity to achieve Self-Realization en masse, a process previously available only to a few dedicated seekers after years of penance.

A Global Spiritual Movement

Unlike other modern spiritual teachers, Shri Mataji insisted that Self-Realization is a living process, and therefore, it cannot be paid for. She traveled across the globe completely free of charge, spreading the message of peace and awakening the Kundalini energy in hundreds of thousands of people.

  • Nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize twice.
  • Honored by the United Nations and multiple global institutions.
  • Sahaj Yoga is now practiced in over 100 countries worldwide.

Core Values & Daily Routine

The foundation of Balshakti is built upon pure moral values and dedicated spiritual routines for a joyous life.

🧘‍♀️ Daily Meditation Routine

Consistency is the key to spiritual growth in Sahaj Yoga.

  • Morning: 10 minutes of meditation to start the day with positive energy.
  • Evening: Foot-soaking (Jal Kriya) to clear daily catchments and relieve stress.
  • Collectivity: Attending weekly center meditation to grow with others.

✨ Innocence & Purity (Shri Ganesha)

The essence of the Mooladhara Chakra.

  • Maintaining a pure heart, free from cunningness.
  • Treating others with absolute honesty and transparency.
  • Preserving childlike joy and avoiding artificial adult complications.

🙏 Respect & Morality

Building a balanced character (The Void / Guru Principle).

  • Always respecting parents, teachers, and elders.
  • Speaking the truth gracefully, without hurting others.
  • Avoiding bad habits, anger, and unnecessary arguments.

🌍 Universal Love & Brotherhood

Awakening the Heart (Anahata Chakra).

  • Respecting all religions and spiritual true masters.
  • Helping others selflessly without expecting anything in return.
  • Protecting nature, animals, and our environment.
