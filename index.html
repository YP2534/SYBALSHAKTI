🙏 About Shri Mataji

Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi

Founder of Sahaj Yoga (1923 – 2011)

Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi was born on March 21, 1923, in Chindwara, India. On May 5, 1970, she opened the Sahasrara Chakra, making it possible for everyone to achieve self-realization through Sahaj Yoga meditation.

  • ✨ Innocence – Be pure in heart like a child
  • ✨ Truth – Always speak and live truthfully
  • ✨ Meditation – Sit quietly and feel the peace within
